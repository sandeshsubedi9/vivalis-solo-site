Submission Guidelines

To maintain quality and trust, all submissions must meet the following standards:

Content Requirements

  • Minimum 800+ words
  • Original, unpublished content only
  • Fact-based and well-structured
  • No exaggerated claims or “miracle” language

Editorial Standards

  • Content must be informational, not promotional
  • No direct product selling or affiliate links
  • Claims must be supported by credible sources where applicable
  • We reserve the right to edit for clarity, tone, and accuracy

Author Bio

Include a short author bio (2–3 sentences) with your background or expertise and an optional website or social profile.

Content Ownership

By submitting content, you confirm you own the rights to the material, it does not infringe on third-party rights, and it complies with applicable guidelines.

Important Note

Vivalis Labs is an independent informational platform, not a medical authority or product manufacturer. All published content must reflect responsible health communication, realistic expectations, and user safety and awareness.
